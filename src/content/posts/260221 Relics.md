  ### Introduction
  The 10,000 Buddha Relics Exhibition is the largest display of Buddhist treasures in the United States, established in 2013. The collection features a vast array of sacred relics sourced from various Asian countries, including Myanmar, Thailand, Sri Lanka, and Vietnam. To ensure an accessible experience for international visitors, translation services and materials are provided in six languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Spanish.
