  ### Design Background
  MUGUHADA was founded to share the understated beauty of Korea with the world. Rather than treating cultural heritage as something to simply observe, the brand reinterprets it through contemporary design and creates objects that can be held, moved, and experienced in everyday life.

  ### Design Direction
  I created MUGUHADA's brand identity and extended it into the ChiMi Chess Knight packaging system. Moving beyond the simple reproduction of traditional ornament, I refined Korean architectural rooflines, floral details, and geometric patterns into a clean contemporary language. Jade and warm orange balance restraint with a playful character, while the modular boxes form connected scenes when rearranged. This carries the brand narrative from the object itself through its display and unboxing experience.
