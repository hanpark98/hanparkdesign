intro: |
  ### Project Background
  For Valley’s second performance in Korea as part of the *Lost in Translation Asia Tour*, my team pitched localized visuals for Korea to Live Nation Korea and led the events. I developed the visual direction, translating the tour’s identity into an experience created specifically for the Korean audience. The localized visuals extended across the merchandise booth and live venue. Posters and slogan banners were also handed out on-site.

  ### Visual Direction
  I approached the localization through two visual directions. The first revisited footage from their debut Korean show at SLSL 2022, transforming a memorable live moment into a series of posters for their return. Using a scene already rooted in the Korean audience’s experience made the campaign locally specific and became part of the localization itself. Valley featured the artwork on their official Instagram Story, and Universal Music Canada reached out to request a copy.

  The second approach “translated” the tour title, *Lost in Translation*, by replacing its English letterforms with similarly shaped Korean consonants and vowels while maintaining the tour’s key visual and color. The resulting system remained recognizable as Valley’s tour identity while giving the Seoul event a distinct local expression. The final artwork was presented to Valley and signed by the band.
