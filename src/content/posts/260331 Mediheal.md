  ### Introduction
  <b>Key Metrics & Data Highlights </b><br><br>
 ‣ <b>99.5% increase in monthly net sales</b> (reaching $45,028.68 in Nov from Oct) by conversion-focused visuals for bundle promos and Gift-With-Purchase events.<br>
 ‣ <b>706% revenue spike</b> triggered on Nov 24th, driven by the BFCM/PLAVE collaboration.<br>
 ‣ <b>48% to 63% open rates</b> (up from a previous average of 15%–30%) achieved through improved visual that focuses on driving strong customer retention and anticipation.<br>
 ‣ Up to <b>10.75% peak CTR</b> (Lilybox Launch) and consistent 4%–5% CTRs during key Nov promos, from the previous baseline of 0.1%–0.4%.<br><br>

 I established cohesive customer journey by unifying visual identities across E-mail/SNS/Shopify Shopfront, while using generative AI to overcome asset limitations and maximize creative diversity.
