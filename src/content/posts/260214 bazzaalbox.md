  ### Project Background
  Bazzaal Box brings the best of K-Beauty to the world through creator-led curations. Unlike traditional subscription services, Bazzaal operates on a “drop” model, with a different product lineup and story for every launch.

  ### Design Direction
  I created a versatile packaging identity that could adapt across changing collections without losing brand recognition. The system balances a share-worthy unboxing experience with the structural durability required for international shipping, giving each drop room for its own theme within one consistent framework.
